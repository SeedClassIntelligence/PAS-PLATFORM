Maternal & child health outreach
Prenatal classes, postpartum groups, pediatric screenings.

The campus's intergenerational model — families, seniors, and youth — makes it a natural hub for maternal and child health programming. Anthem-branded prenatal education classes, postpartum support groups, and well-child screening events capture PPC, W15/W34, and CIS HEDIS measures in a trusted community setting. Behavioral health awareness

Mental health awareness events, substance use disorder education, and peer support programming — hosted in a community room where residents already feel safe. Diabetes education, hypertension management, medication adherence.

Monthly chronic disease management workshops — diabetes self-management education (DSME), hypertension monitoring groups, medication adherence coaching. Senior wellness programming
Fall prevention, medication reviews, social connection.

The intergenerational model brings seniors into daily community life — reducing isolation, the single strongest predictor of avoidable senior ED utilization. Senior wellness events, fall prevention workshops, medication reconciliation reviews, and social programming keep seniors active and engaged. The senior safety value-added benefits in the plan — grab bars, alert devices, medication management tools — are activated at these events.

Network-first referrals — documented in EHR, tracked to completed appointment
Every referral. Zero leakage.
Network
›

Every referral from Jamie's clinic is routed within Anthem's network first. Documented in the EHR at point of order. The CHW then follows each referral — not to a scheduled appointment, to a completed one. Outcomes reported back to the referring provider and logged against the member's care plan.

Why referral completion matters specifically for your quality scores: Follow-Up after ED Visit (FUH) and Follow-Up for Mental Illness (FUM) are both HEDIS measures that depend on completed — not scheduled — follow-up appointments. The CHW closes that loop by accompanying or following through on every referral. That is a direct HEDIS measure driver, not a nice-to-have service.

What this eliminates: Referral leakage — the consistent, measurable loss of attributed members to out-of-network providers, missed appointments, and incomplete care gaps. Every referral that completes in-network is a claim in your network, a quality data point in your records, and a member whose care plan advances rather than stalls.
