The Fellowship

Not networking.
Recognition.

No likes. No followers. No feeds. No algorithm. Fellowship is how PAS holders find each other based on executed work, authored frameworks, and domain authority — not titles, not bios, not keywords. People who have done the work recognizing people who have done the work.

Discover
The system detects alignment between PAS profiles — domain overlap, framework compatibility, execution parallels. Not random suggestions. Real signals from real work.
Inspect
Both PAS holders review each other's exposed modules. You see their Architecture. They see your Evidence. Alignment is confirmed through depth, not profiles.
Connect

How Fellowship Works
No likes. No followers. No feeds. No algorithm.

The fellowship layer allows PAS holders to selectively expose parts of their authority system to identify deep alignment with others. Matching happens based on:

Domain overlap — your Domain Thesis compared to theirs
Framework compatibility — your Architecture compared to theirs
Execution alignment — your Active Implementation intersecting with theirs

When alignment is detected, both PAS holders are notified. They review each other's exposed modules. If alignment is confirmed, contact begins.

This is recognition, not networking. People who have done the work recognizing people who have done the work.
